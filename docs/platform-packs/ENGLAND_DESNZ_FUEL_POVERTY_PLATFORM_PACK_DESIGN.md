# England DESNZ Fuel Poverty Platform Pack

[2026 annual report](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/annual-fuel-poverty-statistics-report-2026)是England Accredited Official Statistics，2026-08-07因benefit-receipt calculation和median-affordability description错误重发。它提供2024 final estimates，并分别提供2025/2026 projections；final、projection、original和corrected release不得互填。

[2026 methodology](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fuel-poverty-statistics-methodology-handbook)定义Low Income Low Energy Efficiency（LILEE）：住宅FPEER为D–G，且扣除housing costs与required energy costs后的income低于poverty line。它基于两年English Housing Survey、住宅physical inspection、required而非actual energy bills及价格/政策模型。LILEE、fuel-poverty gap、10% affordability measure和旧LIHC都是不同indicator。

official route包括annual report、detailed/supplementary/trend XLSX/ODS，以及subregional report/data。national headline与subregional model的reference year、projection standing、geography和statistical designation分开；subregional estimates不得用作individual household识别或精确local trend。

未找到authority-maintained、明确版本/许可且覆盖当前LILEE生产语义的Agent Skill/MCP/OSS。UK Data Service household-level fuel-poverty dataset为separate restricted/special-licence route，不采用。Snapshot/telemetry按`publication/revision × reference/projection year × national/subregional × indicator/model revision × household denominator × count/share/gap × quality/status`记录correction、metric break和zero effects。
